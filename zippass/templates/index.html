how to use - 3 steps

1. drop your .zip in the box above
2. click a quick-start button below - the big password lists are already built in
3. wait - the password shows up in green on this page when found
"exhausted" = that attack tried its whole space and the password is not that type - move to the next button. every guess stays on this computer, nothing is sent anywhere.
